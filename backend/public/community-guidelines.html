Safety & Culture

Community Guidelines

Soulmate is built on respect, authenticity, and kindness. We hold our community to high standards so everyone feels welcome and secure.

✓
Be Respectful & Kind

Treat every member with dignity and courtesy. Disagreements happen, but personal insults, harassment, and condescension have no place on Soulmate.

✗
No Harassment or Bullying

Do not send unsolicited sexual remarks, repetitive unwanted messages, or pressure someone who has declined conversation. Respect personal boundaries.

✓
Authentic Profiles

Use recent, clear photographs of yourself and provide truthful information about your identity. We encourage completing Photo Verification for verified badges.

✗
No Catfishing or Impersonation

Using photos of celebrities, other people, or creating accounts posing as someone else is strictly prohibited and results in an immediate permanent ban.

✓
Protect Your Privacy

Never share financial details, home addresses, or passwords. Use our in-app voice notes and video calls before moving to external messaging apps.

✗
No Scams, Spam, or Commercial Use

Soulmate is for personal relationships. Promoting businesses, affiliate links, financial schemes, cryptocurrency, or soliciting money is strictly forbidden.

✗
Zero Hate Speech or Bigotry

We do not tolerate discrimination or hate speech targeting race, ethnicity, religion, disability, gender identity, or sexual orientation.

✗
No Explicit or Inappropriate Content

Public profile photos must be appropriate for a general audience. Pornographic, violent, or sexually explicit photos are automatically removed.

How We Enforce These Standards

Every user has access to our in-app Report and Block features on every profile and conversation. Our automated safety scanners combined with human moderators review reports within 24 hours.

Violations may result in warnings, temporary profile shadow-bans, or permanent device and IP bans. Severe violations (such as child endangerment, extortion, or credible violent threats) are immediately escalated to law enforcement.
