Google Play Data Safety

Account & Data Deletion Request

In compliance with Google Play and Apple App Store policies, you can request the permanent deletion of your Soulmate account and all associated personal data directly from this web page without needing to open the app.

In-App Instant Deletion (Fastest)

If you currently have the Soulmate mobile app installed, you can delete your account instantly:

  • Open the Soulmate app.
  • Go to Settings ➔ Delete Account.
  • Type DELETE to confirm.

Your profile, chat messages, matches, and media files will be erased immediately.

Web Account Deletion Form

If you have uninstalled the app or lost access to your device, submit the form below. We will send a confirmation code to your registered email to verify ownership before permanently wiping your data.

What Data Gets Deleted?

  • Account Data: Username, email, authentication tokens, and profile attributes.
  • Media Assets: All profile photos, verification selfies, and audio recordings stored in Cloudinary are permanently erased.
  • Social Graph: All sent likes, passes, matches, favorites, and notification records.
  • Messages: All private conversation threads and direct messages.

Data Retention Timeline: Once requested and verified, full erasure is completed within 24 to 48 hours. No residual user data is archived.
